# EtherPets

A little pet that follows you around WEBFISHING: a **wolf**, a **fox**, a **cat** or a **raccoon**, in colours you pick.
It keeps up with you at any speed, waits beside you, and settles down when you stand still for a while.
Works on any server.

*This mod was created with the assistance of AI.*

## The pets

- **Wolf**: trots after you, lies down beside you when you sit, gives a happy whine when petted and
  answers your bark with a short howl.
- **Cat**: hops into your lap and curls up when you sit, purrs when petted and meows back at you.
- **Fox**: slim, with a long snout, big ears and a bushy tail. It walks after you and lies down beside you when you sit.
- **Raccoon**: doesn't walk, it rolls after you in little somersaults. When you sit it climbs up and drapes
  itself over your head. It chatters when petted and when you bark.

## Using it

Press **P** or click the **paw button** (drag it anywhere, it remembers where) to open the pet menu:

- **Wolf / fox / cat / raccoon** buttons pick your pet.
- **Show my pet** turns it on and off.
- **Answer when I bark**: your pet answers your bark or meow (modded species too). **Wait** sets how long
  it waits before answering again, from 15 seconds to 10 minutes.
- **Size** from 50% to 150%.
- Ready-made looks for each pet, plus a colour picker for every part: fur, back, belly, muzzle, paws, tail tip,
  ear backs, eyes, eye shine, nose, mouth, inner ears and tongue (the raccoon's mask, face, front legs and tail rings,
  the cat's whiskers).
- Type a name and press **Save** to keep a palette (up to 12). The palette's name floats above your pet.
  Palettes work on any kind of pet.

## Petting

Walk up to any pet and use the game's interact key on **Pet <name>**. It comes over and you lean down
and stroke its head, sized to fit your character, even a very small one.

## Fetch

You get a **Tennis Ball** in your inventory. Put it on your hotbar, hold it, and click to throw it where you're looking:
hold the mouse button longer (a bar shows how hard, and a dotted arc shows where it will land) and aim higher to throw further. It bounces off walls and rolls to a stop, your pet races
after it, picks it up in its mouth, brings it back and drops it at your feet with a happy wiggle and a happy whine, purr or trill. If it was in your lap
or on your head, it hops down first. A ball it can't reach (in the sea, off a cliff) is let go after a while and it
comes back without it.

The ball can't be sold, mailed or renamed, and it is never written into your save file, so uninstalling the mod
later can't break your inventory.

## Multiplayer

Your pet is shared through the Steam lobby, so everyone with EtherPets sees it exactly as you made it,
and can pet it, and sees it fetch your ball. Players without the mod don't see pets, and nothing changes for them.
